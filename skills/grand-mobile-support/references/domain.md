## Product policy: urgency, not guilt

This queue policy comes from the user's requirements. It is not presented as Grand Mobile's official priority taxonomy.

| Situation | Expected support decision |
| --- | --- |
| Player is being attacked, intentionally run over, abducted or drowned now; a relevant event will disappear before review | Help, `now`; retain missing details as a clarification without delaying recognition of the incident. |
| Account takeover or asset withdrawal is explicitly in progress | Help, `now`. A suspected/previous compromise without current loss activity is normally `high`. |
| Unable to log in, blocked by a bug, missing purchase/property, stuck without imminent danger | Help, `high`; route to an administrator rather than claiming a generic FAQ fixed the issue. |
| Yesterday's attack, completed event, punishment appeal | Help, usually `normal`, unless another explicit risk is continuing. |
| Generic “what does DM mean?” or “how do I buy a vehicle?” | Question; urgency is null. Use a supplied complete verified answer or refer to an administrator. |
| “Urgent”, uppercase, profanity or beginner status without an actual urgent event | These features alone do not establish `now`. |

Victim status and guilt must remain separate: immediate assistance can be justified before evidence is sufficient for a punishment. A clearly ended or negated attack must not be reintroduced by translation. Unknown timing can justify a clarification; it does not justify inventing a current attack.

## Language and glossary scope

The supplied classifier-share package contributes contextual slang and multilingual examples, but its older `question/complaint/violation` schema is not the application's current schema. Definitions selected for a request should be Russian and concise; do not send every translation and every glossary entry each time.

Short forms are ambiguous outside context: ДМить is an action form, Дмитрий can be a nickname, “маты” can be materials or profanity, “ТК” can mean “так как”. Kazakh, Uzbek and Ukrainian negation and quoted speech require clause-level interpretation, not word-by-word substitution. Mixed languages and phonetic spelling are supported goals; this source review does not certify every translation or dialect.

No raw handout examples are reproduced here. The handout taxonomy is explicitly a draft, its categories/priority are not complete human labels for the user's current product, and screenshots are not available for visual verification. Historical admin outcomes must not leak into model input as if they were evidence for a new incident.
